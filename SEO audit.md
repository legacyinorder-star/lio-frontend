Identified SEO Issues
1. Keyword gaps: Many pages fail to include core transactional keywords like “online will,” “write my will online” and “letter of wishes UK.” This limits the site’s ability to rank for high‑intent searches.
2. Heading hierarchy & clarity: Some headings are generic (e.g., “How it works,” “Life is easier with LegacyInOrder”) without context. Others jump from H1 to H3 or H5, making it difficult for search engines to understand structure.
3. Meta titles/descriptions missing or unoptimised: The site’s pages currently use minimal meta titles and often repeat the brand name without keywords. Meta descriptions are either missing or generic.
4. Duplicate or thin content: FAQ answers repeat lines (“We never share your information with third parties without your explicit consent” appears twice【916143480358531†L166-L170】). Pricing tables lack explanatory text.
5. Internal linking: Pages do not link to each other contextually (e.g., the About page doesn’t link to the Wills or Letter of Wishes pages). Internal links help distribute PageRank and improve crawlability.
6. CTAs & conversion language: CTAs such as “Get Started” lack context; stronger, action‑oriented wording could improve conversions.
7. Image alt text: Many images are not described in alt attributes; descriptive alt text with keywords (e.g., “family reviewing will documents”) can improve accessibility and SEO.
8. Local signals & trust: There is little mention of the company’s location (London, England), the jurisdictions served (England & Wales), or regulatory context. Including this information will help satisfy local search intent and trust in YMYL (Your Money or Your Life) content.
